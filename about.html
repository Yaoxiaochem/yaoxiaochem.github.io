About Us

Professional Supplier of Pharmaceutical, Agrochemical and Industrial Chemicals

Company Profile

Hebei Yaoxiao New Materials Technology Co., Ltd. was established in 2018 and is a professional supplier of pharmaceutical intermediates, agrochemical intermediates, industrial chemical raw materials, fine chemicals and new material products. Since its establishment, the company has been committed to providing reliable chemical products and supply chain solutions for customers worldwide. From a small manufacturing facility in its early years, Yaoxiao has gradually developed into an international chemical supplier serving customers in more than 30 countries and regions, including Europe, North America, Southeast Asia, the Middle East and Africa. Our product portfolio covers a wide range of pharmaceutical intermediates, agrochemical intermediates, heterocyclic compounds, amines, aromatic intermediates, industrial chemicals, specialty chemicals and selected new material products. Through continuous product expansion and resource integration, we are able to provide customers with efficient sourcing solutions and stable long-term supply support. Guided by the philosophy of Integrity, Quality and Long-Term Cooperation, Yaoxiao places strict emphasis on quality management, supplier evaluation and customer satisfaction. Every product undergoes rigorous quality verification procedures to ensure compliance with customer specifications and international standards. We believe that reliable quality is the foundation of sustainable business relationships. In addition to product quality, we focus on responsive communication, professional technical support and efficient after-sales service. Our experienced team works closely with customers to provide quotation support, product documentation, logistics coordination and customized sourcing solutions according to different market requirements. Benefiting from China's continuous industrial development, innovation-driven policies and support for advanced manufacturing and new materials, Yaoxiao has achieved steady growth and expanded its global business network year by year. Today, the company continues to strengthen its position as a trusted supplier of pharmaceutical intermediates, agrochemical intermediates, fine chemicals and new material products, creating long-term value for customers around the world.
