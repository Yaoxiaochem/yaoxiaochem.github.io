FAQ

Common questions about chemical product quotations, MOQ, documents, custom sourcing and international shipping.

What is your MOQ?

MOQ depends on product availability, package size and shipping method. For many intermediates, we can support sample orders or small trial quantities before regular purchasing.

What information should I provide for quotation?

Please send product name, CAS number, purity or specification, required quantity, destination country and any document requirements such as COA, SDS or TDS.

Can you provide COA, SDS and TDS?

Yes. COA, SDS/MSDS, TDS and product specification documents can be provided according to the product and batch availability.

What is the usual lead time?

Lead time depends on stock status, production schedule and export arrangement. Stock products can usually be prepared faster, while custom or special products need confirmation case by case.

Which payment terms are available?

Payment terms are confirmed according to order value, product type and cooperation history. Please contact our team for current quotation and payment arrangement.

Do you support custom synthesis or custom sourcing?

Yes. Please provide target product name, CAS number, structure if available, purity, expected quantity and application. Our team will review feasibility and sourcing options.

How do you ship chemicals internationally?

Shipment can be arranged by express, air freight, sea freight or container transportation according to product classification, quantity and destination regulations.

Can you support Europe and North America customers?

Yes. We support customers in Europe, North America, Southeast Asia, the Middle East and Africa with product matching, documents and export coordination.
